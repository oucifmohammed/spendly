Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service, including logging your expenses, generating category breakdowns and spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We may also use your data to communicate important updates about the Service or to improve its features based on how it's used.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, though no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party services for functionality such as hosting or infrastructure, which may process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We do not share your expense data with advertisers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
